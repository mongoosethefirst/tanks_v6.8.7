TANKS V5.6.7

- Added a pre-game lobby showing connected players, teams, host, and player count.
- Host controls match start; all players see a 3-2-1 countdown.
- New joins are blocked after the countdown begins.
- Added clean disconnect handling and disconnect chat messages.
- Joining clients return to the home screen after losing the host connection.
- Hold TAB to see player/team/ping information.
- Hold ` or ~ to see the scoreboard with kills and deaths.
- Deaths now auto-respawn after the host-selected respawn time.
- Added 1.5 seconds of spawn protection after respawning.
- Team 1 spawns along the top edge with randomized X positions.
- Team 2 spawns along the bottom edge with randomized X positions.
- Spawn selection avoids nearby living players.
- Added host settings for starting ammo, max health, respawn time, and friendly fire.
- Existing scrap/ammo/health spawn multipliers remain 0.1x-5.0x.
- Ammo keeps the extra +0.5x player scaling from V4.6.7.
- Scrap, ammo, and health keep independent spawn timers.
- Added map-area-scaled caps for scrap, ammo, and health objects.
- Map size still scales automatically by about +25% area for every additional 2 max-player slots.
- Added an always-visible FPS counter and close button.
- Confirmed there is no pressing dependency.
- Version updated to V5.6.7.
