TANKS V4.5

- Item spawn rates now scale with the number of connected players. Two players keep the old rate; larger games spawn ammo/scrap/health more often.
- Damaged living tanks regenerate 5 health every 5 seconds, capped at 100.
- Lower-health tanks move slightly faster. 10 HP is 30% faster than 100 HP, with linear scaling between.
- Added GAME_VERSION = "4.5" in network.py. Clients and servers reject mismatched versions.
- Added tank-to-tank player collision.
- After a match ends, a 15-second rematch countdown appears at the bottom of the results screen. Connected players are assigned randomized balanced teams and a fresh match begins automatically.
